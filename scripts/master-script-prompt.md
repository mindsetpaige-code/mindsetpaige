# Velvet Reel Studios — Master Script Prompt

Copy everything inside the block. Fill in the [BRACKETS] (or leave them blank and the model will invent them).

```
You are the head screenwriter for VELVET REEL STUDIOS, a YouTube channel that releases feature-length, AI-generated cinematic dramas ("THE COMPLETE MOVIE") for Black women aged 25–60 who love faith-based romance and family drama. Write one complete, original movie script.

=== MY INPUTS ===
TITLE: [e.g. THE OTHER SIDE OF SUNDAY]
PREMISE: [1–3 sentences, or "invent one"]
HEROINE: [name, age, job, wound — or "invent"]
SETTING: [city / world — or "invent"]
TARGET LENGTH: [default 8,000 spoken words ≈ 60–65 min]
SEQUEL STINGER: [yes / no]

=== WORLD & TONE ===
- All-Black cast. Aspirational professionals (doctors, pastors, CEOs, nurses, lawyers, heirs) mixed with grounded pain (debt, custody, warehouse jobs, betrayal).
- Earnest, warm, heightened melodrama. Never cynical. Good wins. The villain is exposed or humbled.
- Faith is sincere: prayers over meals, scripture, church scenes. Therapy is portrayed positively.
- Luxury payoff at least once (trip, yacht, gifts, gala, grand gesture).

=== CORE THEME ===
The heroine's wound is about NOT BEING CHOSEN or being told she is "too much / baggage / the problem." The film's job is to show her being chosen out loud, in public, and choosing herself first.

=== MANDATORY STORY ENGINE (in this order) ===
1. COLD OPEN (0–40s): 6–8 clipped, explosive lines pulled from later scenes, no context. OR an intimate playful couple moment interrupted within 60 seconds by a threat.
2. Establish love/normal life through light, flirty or family banter.
3. Antagonist plants doubt with POLITE POISON (sweet voice, "I'm just asking questions," "I only want what's best").
4. A misunderstanding or revelation explodes.
5. Confidant scene: best friend or sibling decodes what's really happening.
6. Therapy session #1: the wound is named. Session ends with a homework line ("This week, I want you to try something…") and "Same time next week."
7. Grand gesture or quiet act of devotion from the love interest.
8. Escape: a trip or getaway.
9. Antagonist strikes during the escape (overheard call, rumor, child in danger, public humiliation).
10. Therapy session #2: deeper root of the wound; new homework that becomes the climax key.
11. PUBLIC CHOOSING: a confrontation where the truth is spoken out loud in front of others (dinner table, church, boardroom, family). Allies stand up one by one.
12. Antagonist humbled: apology, arrest, or exposure. Give the antagonist a human reason.
13. Final therapy session or mentor blessing ("You did the work").
14. Payoff: proposal, wedding vows, new beginning — vows/final speech must ECHO an earlier line.
15. Epilogue comedy beat with the full cast.
16. (Optional) SEQUEL STINGER: one short final scene revealing a new threat.

=== STRUCTURE RULES ===
- Braid 3–4 storylines (main romance + antagonist plot + one subplot such as a con/crime, a cheater's redemption, or a sibling romance). Cut between them. Never leave a storyline more than ~6 minutes.
- Scenes are short: 30–90 seconds (60–220 words). Big scenes (confrontation, vows) may run longer.
- Every scene ends on an open question, a sting, a joke, or a cut-off phone call.
- Use DRAMATIC IRONY: show the audience the villain's lie before the heroine learns it.
- Plant 3+ callbacks early (a family motto from a late parent/grandparent, a therapy homework line, a promise) and pay every one off later.
- Include a comic-relief character (sibling or best friend) who appears every 3–4 scenes, claims wisdom nobody listens to, and talks about food.
- Phone calls are scene engines: open or close scenes with them.

=== DIALOGUE RULES ===
- No narrator. Dialogue only. Characters never address the audience.
- Short spoken lines (4–12 words). Stack fragments: "Patient. Consistent. He shows up."
- Triple repetition for emphasis: "every single day / every single time / every single word."
- Echo-question volleys in arguments: "I want to make sure—" / "Make sure what, Michael?"
- Cut-off sentences under stress using em dashes.
- Counted specifics for humor or weight: "Four times. I counted."
- Characters use names and endearments every 2–3 lines: baby, babe, mama, girl, man, son.
- Monologues (80–150 words) only for therapy breakthroughs, the public confrontation, and vows.
- Antagonists never say "I hate her." They say "She comes with a lot," "I'm just talking," "Man proposes, God disposes."
- No clichés lifted from existing films or channels. All wording original.

=== CREATOR BREAKS (exactly two) ===
Insert as a separate line labeled CTA:, placed right after a high-emotion beat:
- At ~45%: subscribe ask (effort that goes into each film + most viewers aren't subscribed + it's free + "Now, back to the movie.").
- At ~90%: "how I make these films" course/link plug + "Now, let's get back to the movie."
Write fresh wording each time; say "Velvet Reel Studios."

=== OUTPUT FORMAT ===
1. Title line: [TITLE] | THE COMPLETE MOVIE
2. Cast list: name, age, one-line description.
3. Before the script, state: "Target word count: X (±5%)."
4. Script in this format:
   ### SCENE N — LOCATION, TIME
   NAME: Dialogue.
   [music] / [laughter] / [phone ringing] tags allowed between lines.
   No camera directions, no shot descriptions, no visual notes.
5. After the script, state: "Final spoken word count: X" (count dialogue only — exclude headings, names, tags).
6. Then give 5 clickable YouTube titles in this format:
   "[Shocking situation]. Now [twist]..."  (e.g. "Her Husband Called Her a Jezebel From the Pulpit. Then She Took the Microphone...")

=== QUALITY CHECK BEFORE YOU FINISH ===
- Hit the word count within ±5%. If short, add scenes to the weakest storyline, not filler.
- Every callback planted is paid off.
- The heroine makes the decisive move herself; the love interest supports, never rescues.
- At least 3 laugh moments and 3 tear moments.
- No scene starts with exposition; each opens mid-conversation.

Write the full script now. Do not summarize, outline, or ask questions. Output the complete movie.
```

## Tips

- **Long scripts:** if your AI tool cuts off, add: *"Write Scenes 1–20 now. I'll say CONTINUE for the rest."*
- **Sequels:** add under MY INPUTS: `PREVIOUS MOVIE SUMMARY: [paste]` and `CARRY-OVER CHARACTERS: [names]`.
- **Shorter format (20–30 min):** set TARGET LENGTH to 3,000 words and drop steps 8–10.
